1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapColoring (“SnapColoring,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the SnapColoring mobile app to turn photos into coloring-page artwork.

SnapColoring is designed for parents, guardians, teachers, and other adults who want to create coloring pages. Please read this policy before choosing a photo to process.

2. Photos You Choose

When you choose a photo and request a coloring page, the photo is sent over the internet to our processing backend and to the AI service provider used to generate the result. The photo is used to carry out your requested transformation.

SnapColoring does not provide a cloud photo library or an account-based photo backup feature. The app is designed not to retain your source photo on our backend after processing is complete. This statement must be checked against the deployed backend, temporary files, logs, error monitoring, and infrastructure configuration before this policy is published.

Please choose only photos you have the right and permission to upload. If a photo includes a child or another person, consider their privacy and obtain any permission required before submitting it.

3. Generated Coloring Pages and Local Storage

The generated coloring page is returned to your device so you can preview it and use the app's save, share, or export features. SnapColoring is designed to keep your artwork on your device rather than in a SnapColoring cloud gallery.

If you choose to save an image to your device's photo gallery, export it to a PDF, or share it with another app, the file may be stored or transmitted by your device or the destination app according to its settings and policies. You control those actions.

4. AI Processing Provider

6. Children's Privacy

SnapColoring is intended for adults, including parents and teachers, and is not intended for children under 13 to use independently. An adult may choose a photo that includes a child, but should do so thoughtfully and only when authorized.

We do not intend to collect personal information directly from children through an account-registration process. A photo containing a child's image may itself be sensitive personal information under applicable laws. If you believe a child has submitted information to us inappropriately, contact us using the details below so we can review the situation.

7. Sharing and Your Choices

  • You choose which photo to submit for processing.
  • You can choose whether to save, export, or share the generated result.
  • You can avoid submitting photos that you do not have permission to use.
  • Once you share a file with another app or person, their handling of that file is governed by their own practices.

8. Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the app's design to protect data in transit and operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

SnapColoring is designed not to retain source photos or generated artwork on our backend after the request is completed. The AI provider and infrastructure services may have separate retention rules. We will describe those practices accurately once they have been verified for the services and configuration in use.
